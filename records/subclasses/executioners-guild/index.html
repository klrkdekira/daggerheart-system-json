Executioners Guild

Subclass

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · EXECUTIONERS GUILD · PDF p. 9 · lines 402–426

At a glance

Spellcast Trait
Agility

Description

Play the Executioners Guild if you want to strike down your targets with lethal precision.

First Strike (foundation)

The first time in a scene you succeed on an attack, you deal double damage.

Ambush (foundation)

Your “Marked for Death” feature uses d6s instead of d4s.

Death Strike (specialization)

When you deal Severe damage to a creature, you can mark a Stress to force them to mark an additional Hit Point.

Scorpion’s Poise (specialization)

You gain a +2 bonus to your Evasion against attacks made by a creature you’ve Marked for Death.

True Strike (mastery)

Once per long rest when you fail an attack, you can spend a Hope to make it a success instead.

Backstab (mastery)

Your “Marked for Death” feature uses d8s instead of d6s.
