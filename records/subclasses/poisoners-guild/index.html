Poisoners Guild

Subclass

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · POISONERS GUILD · PDF p. 10 · lines 430–481

At a glance

Spellcast Trait
Knowledge

Description

Play the Poisoners Guild if you want to debilitate your targets with punishing afflictions.

Toxic Concoctions (foundation)

Mark a Stress to place 1d4+1 tokens on this card. When you make a successful weapon attack, you can spend a token to afflict the target with a poison. You know these poisons: - Ghost Petal: The target becomes temporarily Vulnerable. - Grave Spore: The target must also mark a Stress. - Leech Weed: You deal an extra 1d6 damage on this attack. When you take a long rest, clear all unspent tokens.

Poison Compendium (specialization)

You also know these poisons: - Midnight Vine: The target has disadvantage on attack rolls until it marks a Stress to clear this condition. - Gorgon Root: The target becomes temporarily Restrained.

Twin Fang (specialization)

When you afflict a target Marked for Death with a poison you know, you can spend an additional token to also inflict the effect of a second poison you know.

Venomancer (mastery)

You also know these poisons: - Blight Seed: The target gains a −3 penalty to their damage thresholds until the end of the scene. This effect can’t stack. - Fear Leaf: You deal extra damage equal to the result of your Fear Die on this attack. - Corpse Thorn: The target gains disadvantage on reaction rolls until the end of the scene.

Adder’s Blessing (mastery)

You are immune to poisons and other toxins.
