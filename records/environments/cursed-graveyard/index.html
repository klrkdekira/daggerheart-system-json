Cursed Graveyard

Impulses

Description

A sprawling cemetery filled with crumbling headstones, twisted trees possessed by the dead, and crypts overgrown with moss and ivy.

Room for One More (Passive)

If a PC marks their last HP while in the graveyard, they become a Specter instead of making a death move. While a Specter, a PC can communicate with the living but can’t interact with the physical world, and they must stay within Close range of their corpse or cross through the veil of death. This condition is cleared when the PC clears a HP. What new information or experience does a PC gain from becoming a specter? How can the party reunite the PC’s spirit with their flesh? What happens if their ritual goes wrong?

Hallowed Ground (Passive)

At the center of the graveyard is a seemingly undisturbed chapel. On a successful Instinct Roll, a PC can discover this structure is sanctified against the cemetery’s denizens. While inside the chapel, the PCs have advantage on action rolls to fight or drive off spirits and undead. Who sanctified this place, and what happened to them? What holy relics can be found here?

Misty Miasma (Action)

Spend a Fear to fill an area within Far range with a low-lying ectoplasmic fog that seeps up through the soggy earth. When a PC rolls with Fear while in the miasma, they must mark a Stress. What horrors do the PCs see in the fog? What disorienting smell does the miasma emit?

The Dead Arise (Action)

Spend a Fear to have undead hands burst forth from the ground beneath a PC. The target must succeed on an Agility or Presence Reaction Roll or mark a Stress and become temporarily Restrained as hands grasp them. Who were these people in life? Why were they buried here? Why won’t they stay dead?

The Dead Don’t Die Easy (Reaction)

When an adversary would mark their last HP, roll a d6. On a result of 5 or 6, the adversary doesn’t mark it. Who commanded this land to reject death? How do the dead come back different here?
