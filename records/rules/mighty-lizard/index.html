Mighty Lizard

Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · MIGHTY LIZARD · PDF p. 17 · lines 1045–1057

Source text

*(Alligator, Crocodile, Gila Monster, etc.)* *Instinct +2 | Evasion +1* *Melee Instinct d10+7 phy* Gain advantage on: attack, sneak, track Physical Defense: You gain a +3 bonus to your damage thresholds. Snapping Strike: When you succeed on an attack against a target within Melee range, you can spend a Hope to clamp that opponent in your jaws, making them temporarily Restrained and Vulnerable.
