Mythic Beast

Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · MYTHIC BEAST · PDF p. 18 · lines 1169–1178

Source text

*(Upgraded Tier 1 or Tier 2 Options)* Evolved: Pick a Tier 1 or Tier 2 Beastform option and become a larger, more powerful version of that creature. While you’re in this form, you retain all traits and features from the original form and gain the following bonuses: - A +9 bonus to damage rolls - A +2 bonus to the trait used by this form - A +3 bonus to Evasion - Your damage die increases by one size (d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10, etc.)
