Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · RUNNING AN ADVENTURE · REGROUP · PDF p. 92 · lines 4835–4841

Source text

​​Choice: When a battle begins, choose a point within Far range. Trigger: All PCs have marked all of their Armor Slots. Effect: Teleport all PCs and this NPC to the chosen spot and clear an Armor Slot on each target.
