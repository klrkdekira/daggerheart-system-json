Step 3: Choose Their Companion Experience

Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · STEP 3: CHOOSE THEIR COMPANION EXPERIENCE · PDF p. 21 · lines 1386–1394

Source text

Create two Experiences for your companion based on their training and the history you have together. Start with +2 in both Experiences. Whenever you gain a new Experience, your companion also gains one. All new Experiences start at +2. Example Companion Experiences Bold Distraction, Expert Climber, Fetch, Friendly, Guardian of the Forest, Horrifying, Intimidating, Loyal Until the End, Navigation, Nimble, Nobody Left Behind, On High Alert, Protective, Royal Companion, Scout, Service Animal, Trusted Mount, Vigilant, We Always Find Them, You Can’t Hit What You Can’t Find
