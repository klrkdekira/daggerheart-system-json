Subclasses

Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · SUBCLASSES · PDF p. 9 · lines 363–372

Source text

Each class is divided into two subclasses, each of which further defines and highlights one aspect of its class archetype. Your chosen subclass can grant the following: - Spellcast Trait: This is the trait used on all Spellcast rolls. - Foundation Feature: This is a unique starting feature that establishes the identity and strengths of the subclass. You gain your chosen subclass's Foundation at character creation. - Specialization Feature: This is an advanced feature that can be gained on level up. For some subclasses, the specialization feature grants a new ability; for others, it expands or enhances a feature you already acquired through a class or subclass. - Mastery Feature: The mastery feature is a subclass’s most powerful feature, which can be gained at higher levels. For some subclasses, the mastery feature unlocks an extraordinary new ability; for others, it’s the impressive culmination of their subclass’s core feature. When you acquire a Foundation, Specialization, or Mastery, take its card for use as a reference during play.
