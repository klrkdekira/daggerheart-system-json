Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · POWERFUL BEAST · PDF p. 16 · lines 957–969

Source text

*(Bear, Bull, Moose, etc.)* *Strength +3 | Evasion +1* *Melee Strength d10+4 phy* Gain advantage on: navigate, protect, scare Rampage: When you roll a 1 on a damage die, you can roll a d10 and add the result to the damage roll. Additionally, before you make an attack roll, you can mark a Stress to gain a +1 bonus to your Proficiency for that attack. Thick Hide: You gain a +2 bonus to your damage thresholds.
