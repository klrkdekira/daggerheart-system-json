Elemental Kin

Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · ELEMENTAL KIN · PDF p. 33 · lines 2199–2207

Source text

Elemental kin are humanoid mortals descended from powerful supernatural entities known as elementals—embodiments of the fundamental forces that shape the Mortal Realm (such as earth, air, fire, and water). These “kin” manifest forces differently, with some appearing more or less humanoid than others, but they all have a lineage that grants them unique ways to harness and influence their elemental heritage. At birth, elemental kin closely resemble the element they embody, then become increasingly humanoid as they mature through early childhood. As they grow, their appearance manifests unique characteristics that identify them as elemental kin, such as crystal formations across a stonekin’s shoulders or a skykin’s ever-present breeze tousling their hair. While adult elemental kin are predominantly flesh and blood, small portions of their bodies momentarily shift into an elemental state in times of great emotion. Elemental kin with limb differences, or who lost a limb at some point in their life, can also manipulate a small amount of their element to help them accomplish a variety of tasks. Some elemental kins’ physical characteristics are passed down through families, such as one family of earthkin bearing shifting skin with the texture of sand, while another bears marble patterns. Additionally, families often develop their own unique communication styles that use their elemental influence. For example, emberkin might express excitement with flares of fire from their eyes, while tidekin might use currents of color over their bodies to communicate subtle messages. Though their lifespans vary, all elemental kin return to their element upon their death.
