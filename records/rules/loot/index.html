Rule

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MECHANICS · LOOT · PDF p. 75–79 · lines 4019–4184

Source text

Loot comprises any consumables or reusable items the party acquires. ITEMS Items can be used until sold, discarded, or lost. You can use the tables below to generate items ahead of a game or during a session. Choose the desired rarity, roll the associated number of d12s, add their values together (if needed), and then reference the table for the item that matches that value. - Common (1d12 or 2d12): Common items might be found at an abandoned camp or readily available at a local shop. - Uncommon (2d12 or 3d12): Uncommon items might be found in limited supply in a shop, kept in a protected place in a camp, or offered as part of a reward for a job. - Rare (3d12 or 4d12): Rare items might be kept under lock and key in a shop, offered as the sole reward for a job, or discovered among a powerful NPC’s possessions. - Legendary (4d12 or 5d12): Legendary items might be the only item of their kind, a reward for an incredibly difficult or dangerous job, or a powerful adversary’s most precious and guarded treasure.
