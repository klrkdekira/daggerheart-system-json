Emberite Shard

Consumable

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MECHANICS · Additional Consumables · PDF p. 83 · lines 4325–4325

At a glance

Set
expansion
Roll
35

Description

Choose a point within Far range. All targets within Close range of that point must succeed on a Reaction Roll (16) or take 3d6 magic damage and become temporarily Ablaze. While Ablaze, a creature must roll a d4 whenever they make an action roll. On a result of 1, they mark a Hit Point. On a result of 4, they clear the Ablaze condition.
