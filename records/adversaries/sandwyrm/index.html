Description

A gargantuan burrowing worm that has armor plating and gnashing jaws. This intelligent and opportunistic hunter can swim through sand as quickly as a cheetah runs on land.

Venomous Tail Stinger (Action)

Mark a Stress to attack a target within Close range. On a success, the target takes 3d10+2 physical damage and must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll or become Restrained and Vulnerable until they roll with Hope.

Devour (Action)

Make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, the Sandwyrm Swallows the target. While Swallowed, the target is Restrained and marks a HP when the Sandwyrm is spotlighted. The Sandwyrm disgorges all Swallowed creatures when it takes Major or greater damage.

Hungry, Not Stupid (Reaction)

When the Sandwyrm marks half its HP or Swallows a creature, roll a d6. On a result of 3 or lower, the Sandwyrm retreats to safety.
