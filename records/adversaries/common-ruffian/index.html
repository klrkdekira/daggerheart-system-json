Description

A local laborer or artisan pushed by circumstances to assail others.

Group Attack (Action)

Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Ruffians within Close range of them. The Ruffians move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 1d8 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

Survival Instinct (Reaction)

When the Ruffian marks half their HP, roll a d6. On a result of 4 or higher, the Ruffian flees the scene. Otherwise, you gain a Fear.
