Description

A seeker of secret knowledge hidden away in a tome-filled tower.

Relentless (3) (Passive)

The Archmage can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

Orbiting Aegis (Passive)

When the Archmage appears, they conjure six discs of magical energy that orbit around them. Place a d6 Aegis Die on this stat block with the 6 value facing up. When a PC makes a successful attack against the Archmage, roll a d6. If the result is equal to or less than the value of the Aegis Die, tick the Aegis Die down and ignore the damage. A PC can make a Knowledge Roll to tick down the Aegis Die by 1 on a success or by 2 on a success with Hope.

Force Salvo (Action)

Spend any number of Fear and roll that many d4s. The Archmage unleashes a number of magical homing force bolts equal to the total result. For each bolt, a creature within Far range marks a HP. The same creature can be targeted by up to three force bolts.

Fireball (Action)

Mark any number of Stress to target the same number of PCs within Far range. Each target must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll or take 4d10 magic damage.

Counterspell (Reaction)

When a PC succeeds on a Spellcast Roll, you can spend a Fear to have the Archmage make a reaction roll with a Difficulty equal to the Spellcast Roll’s result. On a success, the PC’s Spellcast Roll fails instead, and you place a token on this stat block. Using this feature again costs additional Fear equal to the number of tokens on this stat block. You can’t Counterspell a critical success.
