Description

A half-fish, half-human creature psychically bound to serve the Deep Dwellers.

Fish-Tailed (Passive)

The Merfolk is Vulnerable out of the water.

Gang Up (Passive)

The Merfolk gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls against targets within Melee range of one or more allies.

“Get Over Here!” (Reaction)

When the Merfolk makes a successful standard attack against a target beyond Melee range, you can mark a Stress to pull them into Melee range. If you do, you can spend a Fear to make a standard attack with advantage against the target.
