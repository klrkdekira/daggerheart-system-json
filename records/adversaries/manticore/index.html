Description

A legendary beast that has a human’s face, lion’s body, scorpion’s tail, and dragon’s wings.

Relentless (3) (Passive)

The Manticore can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

Riddle Me This (Passive)

The Manticore is obsessed with puzzles and can be distracted by brain teasers, word games, or other semi-intellectual diversions.

Winged (Passive)

While airborne, the Manticore gains a +2 bonus to their Difficulty and attack rolls.

Pouncing Attack (Action)

Spend a Fear to have the Manticore pounce into Melee range of a target within Far range and make an attack against them. On a success, deal 3d20+6 physical damage, and the target becomes Shaky until they spend a Hope. While Shaky, the target makes attack rolls with disadvantage.

Paralyzing Tail Spike (Action)

Mark a Stress to make an attack roll against a target within Close range. On a success, the target takes 3d8 physical damage and becomes Restrained until they overcome the toxin with a successful Strength Roll.

Locking Jaws (Reaction)

When the Manticore succeeds on a standard attack against a target within Melee range, you can mark a Stress to lock their jaws onto the target, making the target Restrained and Vulnerable until they escape with a successful Strength Roll (19).
