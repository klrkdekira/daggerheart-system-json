Description

An empty suit of full plate armor that glows with a faint corona of ghostly light.

Tireless (Passive)

The Armor can’t be forced to mark Stress. When an effect would cause it to mark Stress, the Armor ignores that part of the effect.

Clatter & Recombobulate (Reaction)

Once per scene when the Armor is defeated, you can spend a Fear to reactivate it. Clear 2 HP and immediately spotlight it.
