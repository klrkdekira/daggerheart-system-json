Description

The echo of a lost soul in the form of an ectoplasmic shadow.

Incorporeal (Passive)

The Phantom has resistance to physical damage and can move through solid objects.

Fear Aura (Passive)

A PC who takes the spotlight within Very Close range of the Phantom must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (10) or mark a Stress.

Lingering Haunt (Reaction)

Countdown (1d6). When the Phantom is defeated, you can spend a Fear to activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC rolls with Fear. When it triggers, clear the Phantom’s HP and immediately spotlight them.
