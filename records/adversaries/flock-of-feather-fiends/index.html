Description

A flock of undead crow corpses, eyes aglow with necroplasmic light.

Horde (1d6+1) (Passive)

When the Flock has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 1d6+1 magic damage instead.

Maddening Cacophony (Action)

Mark a Stress to force each PC within Very Close range to make an Instinct Reaction Roll. Targets who fail must spend a Hope or become Vulnerable until they deal damage to the Flock or the Flock is defeated.
