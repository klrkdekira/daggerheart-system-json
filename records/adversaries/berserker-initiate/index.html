Description

A raging warrior who imbibes ritualistic concoctions to enhance their form with aspects of a predatory beast.

Pack Fury (Passive)

The Initiate gains a +1 bonus to their attack rolls for each ally within Very Close range of their attack’s target.

Berserker Rage (Reaction)

The first time in a scene the Initiate marks their last Stress, they gain a +4 bonus to damage rolls.
