Description

A wolf-headed humanoid cursed by the gods to prowl the swamps in search of blood.

Relentless (3) (Passive)

The Rugaru can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

Child of Night (Passive)

While in moonlight, the Rugaru gains a +2 bonus to their Difficulty.

Bloodthirsty (Passive)

When a PC marks HP from the Rugaru’s standard attack, add a token to this stat block. The Rugaru gains a bonus to attack rolls equal to the number of tokens on this stat block. Clear all tokens when the Rugaru takes Severe damage.

Howl at the Moon (Action)

Mark a Stress to force each PC within Far range to make a Presence Reaction Roll (15). Targets who fail lose a Hope. Gain a Fear for each Hope lost. If a PC has no Hope, they must mark a Stress and become Vulnerable until they roll with Hope.

Flesh Ripper (Action)

Mark a Stress to make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, deal 1d12+2 direct physical damage.
