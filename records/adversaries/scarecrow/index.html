Description

A bundle of straw bound into a humanoid shape, clothed in rags, and animated by a trapped soul.

Made of Straw (Passive)

The Scarecrow has resistance to physical damage but takes double damage from fire.

Terror Vision (Action)

Spend a Fear to have the Scarecrow lock eyes with a PC within Very Close range, dealing 2d8 magic damage and making them Vulnerable until they roll with Hope.
