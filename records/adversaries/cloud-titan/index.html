Description

A devastatingly attractive, blue-skinned goliath dressed in the finest silks and dripping with jewelry. They luxuriate in the finer things and dwell in a sky palace filled with treasure.

Lightning Strike (Action)

Mark a Stress to make an attack against a target within Far range. On a success, deal 4d10+14 magic damage and roll a d6. On a result of 4 or higher, the lightning bounces, and the Titan makes this attack against a different target within Very Close range of the previous target. Each time the lightning bounces, decrease the damage it deals by 1d10.

Mist Weaver (Action)

Spend a Fear and choose a point within Far range. The Titan conjures a thick fog over the area within Close range of that point. The Titan is Hidden to enemies within and on the other side of the fog and has advantage on attacks against creatures inside it. The fog lasts until the Titan marks HP.

Nebulous Transformation (Action)

Mark a Stress to have the Titan transform into a misty cloud that fills a Very Close area. While in this form, the Titan takes double magic damage and has immunity to physical damage. This form lasts until the Titan chooses to drop out of it or takes Severe damage.

Unleash the Menagerie (Action)

Once per scene, mark any number of Stress to summon an equal number of Griffins, which appear at Close range.

Wind Worker (Action)

Spend a Fear to have the Titan blast wind at a target within Close range. The target takes 4d6+8 physical damage and is pushed back to Far range from their current position.

Double or Nothing (Reaction)

When you would gain a Fear from an attack against the Titan, you can roll a d6. On an odd result, you don’t gain the Fear. On an even result, you gain 2 Fear instead of 1.
