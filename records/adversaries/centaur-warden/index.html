Description

A half-human, half-horse guardian who patrols the borders of idyllic lands uncorrupted by the touch of civilization.

Flanking Maneuver (Passive)

When the Warden makes a standard attack against a target within Melee range of one or more of the Warden’s allies, the Warden gains a +2 bonus to the attack roll.

Eye of the Sage (Action)

Mark a Stress to make a standard attack. The Warden has advantage on the attack roll and critically succeeds on a roll of 19–20.

Trample (Action)

Spend a Fear to have the Warden gallop up to Far range in a straight line. Each target in their path must make an Agility Reaction Roll. Targets who succeed take 1d6+2 physical damage. Targets who fail take double damage and must mark a Stress.

Quick Volley (Reaction)

When the Warden makes a standard attack, you can mark any number of Stress to target an equal number of additional creatures within range.
