Description

A snake-tailed humanoid monster who stalks the night to absorb the life energy of sleeping people.

Constriction (Action)

Mark a Stress to have the Lamia wrap their tail around a PC within Melee range and squeeze. The target must succeed on a Finesse Reaction Roll or become Constricted until the Lamia moves or takes Major or greater damage. While Constricted, the target is Restrained and must mark a Stress when the Lamia takes the spotlight.

Life Leech (Action)

Mark a Stress to force a Vulnerable or Restrained target within Melee range to mark 1d4−1 HP. The Lamia then clears an equal number of HP.

Sleep Toxin (Reaction)

When the Lamia makes a successful standard attack against a PC, you can spend a Fear to make them Vulnerable until they succeed on a Instinct Roll (19).
