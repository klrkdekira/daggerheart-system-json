Description

A troop of fist-sized fey creatures who have needle-sharp teeth and conical red caps sealed on their heads with candle wax.

Horde (1d4+2) (Passive)

When the Biters have marked half or more of their HP, their standard attack deals 1d4+2 physical damage instead.

Ankle Weights (Passive)

A PC must mark a Stress to move out of the Biters’ Melee range.
