Description

A cloud of blood-sucking bats.

Horde (2d6) (Passive)

When the Swarm has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 2d6 physical damage instead.

Blinding Multitude (Passive)

The Swarm is so thick it blocks the vision of anyone it interacts with. Creatures within Melee range have disadvantage on attacks made against adversaries other than the Swarm.

Bloodsuckers (Reaction)

When the Swarm deals damage to a target, you can mark a Stress to have the Swarm feed. The target marks an additional HP, and the Swarm clears a HP.
