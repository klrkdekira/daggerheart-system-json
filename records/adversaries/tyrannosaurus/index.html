Description

A huge carnivorous dinosaur that walks on two legs.

Earth-Shaking Roar (Action)

Spend a Fear to force each PC within Close range to make a Presence Reaction Roll. On a failure, they must choose to lose a Hope or mark 1d4 Stress.

Stomp (Action)

Mark a Stress to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, the target takes 3d12 direct physical damage.

Crushing Bite (Reaction)

When the Tyrannosaurus makes a successful standard attack, you can spend a Fear to force the target to mark an additional HP.
