Description

A clutch of newly hatched dragons. As newborns, they’re furious, hungry, and stupid.

Horde (2d6+5) (Passive)

When the Wyrmlings have marked half or more of their HP, their standard attack deals 2d6+5 physical damage instead.

Ravenous (Reaction)

Once per GM turn when a PC marks a HP from an attack made by the Wyrmlings, you can spend a Fear to spotlight the Wyrmlings again.

Overwhelm (Reaction)

When the Wyrmlings take Minor damage from an attack within Melee range, you can mark a Stress to make a standard attack with advantage against the attacker.
