Description

A goliath clad in animal skins and surrounded by a swarm of insects.

Relentless (3) (Passive)

The Titan can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

Momentum (Passive)

When the Titan makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

Gullible (Passive)

PCs have advantage on action rolls to trick or emotionally manipulate the Titan.

Bug Swarm (Passive)

PCs are Vulnerable while within Very Close range.

Mighty Swing (Action)

Spend a Fear to make a standard attack against all targets within Very Close range.
