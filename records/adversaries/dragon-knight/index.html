Description

A knight clad in dragonplate armor and a helmet in the shape of a dragon’s head.

High Ground (Passive)

The Knight gains advantage on attacks made against targets below them.

Leaping Strike (Action)

Mark a Stress to have the Knight leap into Melee range of a target within Far range and make a standard attack against them. On a success, the attack deals an extra 1d12 damage, and the target must mark an Armor Slot without receiving its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).

Comeback (Reaction)

When the Knight fails a standard attack, you can spend a Fear to reroll the attack with advantage.
