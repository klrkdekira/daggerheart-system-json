Description

An unfortunate undead laborer chosen by lottery to wall up their empress’s tomb from the inside.

Minion (5) (Passive)

The Stonemason is defeated when they take any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Stonemason, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range their attack would succeed against.

Group Attack (Action)

Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Entombed Stonemasons within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 5 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

The Work Never Ends (Reaction)

When the Stonemason is defeated by an attack that deals physical damage, you can spend a Fear to clear the Stonemason’s HP and Stress.
