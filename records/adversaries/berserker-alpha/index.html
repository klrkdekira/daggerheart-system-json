Description

A veteran commander who quaffs magic elixirs to change into a beast and lead their packmates on a hunt.

Pack Fury (Passive)

The Alpha gains a +1 bonus to their attack rolls for each ally within Very Close range of their attack’s target.

Bark at the Moon (Action)

Once per scene, spend a Fear to have each ally within Close range of the Alpha clear a HP and a Stress.

Berserker Rage (Reaction)

The first time in a scene the Alpha marks their last Stress, they gain a +6 bonus to damage rolls.
