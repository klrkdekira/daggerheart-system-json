Description

A gray-skinned goliath who lives underground and specializes in crafting powerful items inlaid with magical runes.

Relentless (3) (Passive)

The Titan can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.

Skillful Strike (Passive)

Before the Titan makes an attack, you can mark a Stress and roll a d6. On a result of 3 or higher, the target’s Evasion is halved against this attack.

Sunlight Sickness (Passive)

When the Titan first appears above ground, roll a d4. Mark a number of Stress equal to the result. The Titan gains a bonus to their attack and damage rolls equal to the number of Stress marked.

Hammer Smash (Action)

When a PC marks HP from the Titan’s standard attack, you can spend a Fear to force the target to mark an Armor Slot without gaining its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).
