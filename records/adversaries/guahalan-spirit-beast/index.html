Description

A brightly colored spirit in the shape of an animal.

Minion (5) (Passive)

The Spirit Beast is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Spirit Beast, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

Ethereal Form (Passive)

The Spirit Beast has resistance to physical damage.

Group Attack (Action)

Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Guahalan Spirit Beasts within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 5 magic damage each. Combine this damage.

Beast Bomb (Reaction)

When the Spirit Beast is defeated, it explodes in white flame and deals 5 magic damage to creatures within Melee range.
