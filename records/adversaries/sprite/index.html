Description

A tiny winged warrior that attacks trespassers with poison-tipped arrows.

Minion (5) (Passive)

The Sprite is defeated when they take any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Sprite, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

Art of Invisibility (Action)

Spend a Fear to make the Sprite Hidden until they attack.

Dream Petal Poison (Action)

When the Sprite succeeds on a standard attack, you can spend a Fear to make the target Sleepy until the target takes damage. While Sleepy, the target must make a Presence Reaction Roll before they make an action roll. On a failure, they must mark 2 Stress or fall asleep until they take damage.
