Description

A lavishly adorned skeleton that has an endless appetite for gold.

Attracted to Wealth (Passive)

PCs carrying at least a bag of gold gain advantage on Presence Rolls made to influence the Catrin.

The Weight of Opulence (Action)

Spend a Fear to turn the Catrin’s gaze toward the PC carrying the most gold. The target must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll or lose a Hope for each bag of gold they’re carrying. If the PC loses their last Hope or has no Hope to lose, they mark Stress instead.

Expensive Failure (Reaction)

When a PC within Melee range fails an action roll, you can mark a Stress to have the Catrin brazenly steal a handful of gold from them.
