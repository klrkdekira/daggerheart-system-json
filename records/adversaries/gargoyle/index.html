Description

A magically animated statue resembling a terrifying beast or demon.

Unliving (Passive)

The Gargoyle has resistance to magic damage.

Swooping Strike (Action)

Choose a point within Far range. The Gargoyle moves to that point and makes a standard attack against one target along its path.

Petrifying Slash (Reaction)

When the Gargoyle deals damage, the target must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll or become Vulnerable. If the target is already Vulnerable, they become Restrained. If the target is already Vulnerable and Restrained, they must make a death move. The PC can spend a Hope per condition to clear it.
