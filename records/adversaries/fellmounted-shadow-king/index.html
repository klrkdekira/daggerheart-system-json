Description

The shade of a forgotten ruler, cursed to patrol the skies atop a winged eel in the service of an even greater evil.

Fellmount (Passive)

While the Shadow King is on his mount, he gains a +2 bonus to his Difficulty. When the Shadow King takes Severe damage, he’s knocked from his mount. If the Shadow King is unmounted, his mount disappears until you mark a Stress to summon it again.

Light and Shadow (Passive)

The Shadow King has advantage on attack rolls while in shadow or darkness and disadvantage on attack rolls while in bright light.

Relic Hunter (Passive)

The Shadow King has advantage on attack rolls against PCs who have magic weapons or armor equipped.

Hellsong (Action)

Spend 2 Fear to have the Shadow King’s mount unleash a spine-chilling screech that echoes over the battlefield. Each PC within Far range must make a Presence Reaction Roll (18). Targets who succeed must mark a Stress. Targets who fail must mark a Stress and lose a Hope. If a PC can’t lose a Hope, you gain 2 Fear.

Air Support (Reaction)

Three times per scene, you can spend a Fear to have an ally within Far range reroll a failed attack with advantage.
