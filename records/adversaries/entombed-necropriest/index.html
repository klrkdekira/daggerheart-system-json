Description

A necromancer charged with preserving the empress’s life beyond death.

Invigorate (Action)

Mark a Stress to choose up to three allies within Far range. Each one gains advantage on their next attack roll.

Last Grasp (Action)

Spend a Fear to choose a PC within Far range. Skeletal hands burst out of the ground at the PC’s feet and Restrain them until the PC breaks free with a successful Strength Roll. When a target Restrained in this way spends Hope, you gain a Fear.

Chill Touch (Action)

Mark a Stress to make a standard attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, the target takes standard damage and must mark 1d4 Stress. If the target marks their last Stress from this attack, you gain a Fear.
