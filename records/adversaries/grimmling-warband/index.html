Horde (1d4+1) (Passive)

When the Warband has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 1d4+1 physical damage instead.

Cowardly (Reaction)

When the Warband marks its last Stress or an allied Leader is defeated, roll a d6. On a result of 2 or lower, the Warband flees the scene.
