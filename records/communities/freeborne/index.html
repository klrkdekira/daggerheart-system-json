Freeborne

Community

Daggerheart SRD 2.0 · CORE MATERIALS · FREEBORNE · PDF p. 38–39 · lines 2466–2478

Description

Being part of a freeborne community means you’re from a collective that once lived under tyrannical rule but is now liberated. Though no two freeborne enclaves are the same, all are unified by the experience of fighting to preserve their culture while others tried to eradicate it. Whether due to their religion, resources, location, or culture, freeborne groups were once subjugated by another community. For some, this subjugation might have only recently ended; for others, even if their freedom has been long-won, the scars of tyranny can last, bonding together families and neighbors for decades or even generations. Many freeborne collectives unify in and rebuild the lands reclaimed from their oppressors or in a homeland they were taken from. Others see “home” as a collection of people who care for one another, no matter where they reside. Many freeborne passionately celebrate their unique holidays, art, food, decor, and other customs, whether they’re enjoying long-held traditions or new practices born from liberation. Their past experiences often forge in them a fierce protectiveness of their community and an equally strong fervor to celebrate their culture. Freeborne are often optimistic, guarded, courageous, joyful, subversive, and traumatized.

Unbound

Once per session when you roll with Fear, you can change it into a roll with Hope instead.
